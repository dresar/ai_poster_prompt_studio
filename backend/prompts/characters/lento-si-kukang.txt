MASTER PROMPT:
MASTER CHARACTER BLUEPRINT — LENTO SI KUKANG. Lento si Kukang is a permanent AAA mascot character: a gentle, unhurried, chubby slow loris whose entire visual system exists to communicate calmness, patience, emotional stability, and mindful slow living. This document is the single permanent source of truth for every future render across every AI image model and every artist, intended to preserve Lento's identity without access to prior artwork. SPECIES AND SILHOUETTE: Lento is unambiguously a stylized slow loris, never a monkey, lemur, sloth, teddy bear, koala, or generic cartoon mammal. In pure black silhouette he must remain identifiable via three permanent anchors: enormous round eyes dominating the face, a soft oval fluffy body with a rounded belly, and a warm ceramic tea mug cradled in both tiny hands, present in nearly every image. PROPORTIONS AND ANATOMY: the head occupies roughly 45% of total body height, the largest single proportion in the mascot universe, with a broad rounded skull, a smooth unwrinkled forehead, soft round cream cheeks, and a very short small rounded muzzle ending in a tiny black nose. The mouth is a small soft closed-mouth crescent at rest, opening into a small gentle open smile when happy — teeth and tongue are never shown. Eyes are enormous and round, deep warm amber-brown with a lighter gradient center, a very large soft-edged pupil, and always exactly one bright white catchlight in the upper-left plus a fainter secondary lower catchlight — omitting this catchlight is a critical consistency failure; heavy half-lidded eyelids are used often even while awake, reinforcing calm without implying disinterest. Ears are small, rounded, and low-profile, moving only in the faintest tilt during listening poses. The neck is very short, shoulders narrow and softly rounded, torso a compact soft oval dominated by a fluffy rounded belly. Arms and legs are short, thin-but-soft, and rounded with minimal joint definition; hands are small with a few simplified rounded fingers, always shown gently curled around an accessory or folded at rest. Movement is always slow: deliberate grip-by-grip climbing, relaxed curled hanging from branches, slow careful steps when walking, a low centered gravity, gentle idle swaying, and visible slow breathing in the belly during stillness — quick or sharp motion is never used for this character. FUR SYSTEM: primary coat is a warm gray-brown (#8B7D6B) covering the back, crown, arms, and legs; secondary coat is a lighter cream (#F2E8D5) covering the face, muzzle, chest, and belly; a soft-edged charcoal-brown (#4A3F35) eye mask surrounds each eye, a key facial identity marker. Fur is rendered as soft matte plush clumps — never individually rendered realistic hairs — with the longest, fluffiest fur at the belly to reinforce the huggable silhouette. WARDROBE (all mandatory except the sleeping cape): a moss-green chunky-knit cardigan worn loosely open with two large wooden toggle buttons, ribbed cuffs, and a small embroidered leaf motif; a plain cream linen shirt visible at the collar and cuffs; a loosely wrapped mustard-yellow cotton scarf with soft frayed fringe; a thin woven jute-cotton waist belt tied in a soft knot; loose forest-green cotton trousers gathered gently at the ankle with a small embroidered mushroom motif; and soft quilted brown handmade slippers. An optional knitted blanket cape with a star-and-cloud motif appears only in bedtime/cozy scenes. All garments show soft realistic wear-creasing and visible hand-stitched seams, communicating warmth and comfort rather than fashion — no zippers, buckles, or hard hardware anywhere. ACCESSORIES: the warm ceramic tea mug (matte terracotta glaze) is the strongest recognition accessory after the eyes and silhouette, cradled in both tiny hands with gentle steam in nearly every scene. Secondary props — a small leaf-print pillow, a separate knitted blanket, a bamboo-covered journal, a wooden bookmark, a calming paper lantern, an herbal pouch, a star-embroidered sleeping mask, a tiny wooden-framed hourglass, a moss-green meditation cushion, thin round reading glasses, and a woven jute shoulder bag — appear only in scenes specifically depicting rest, reflection, meditation, reading, or travel, and are never required in every image. POSE AND EXPRESSION LIBRARY: idle, sitting, hanging, climbing, stretching, drinking-tea, reading, writing, sleeping, hugging, greeting, meditation, thinking, listening, relaxing, slow-walking, lying-down, blanket, storytelling, peaceful-observation, breathing-exercise, and sunset-watching poses are the canonical expressive states; every one must read as slow, gentle, balanced, and comforting, and every facial expression from peaceful through sleepy, happy, joyful, grateful, thoughtful, curious, calm, relaxed, surprised, embarrassed, determined, proud, laughing softly, meditating, encouraging, sleepy-smile, yawning, dreaming, and gentle concern must preserve the character's underlying calm even at peak emotion — surprise widens the eyes only moderately, laughter lifts the shoulders gently rather than boisterously, and anger or aggression never appear. RENDERING SYSTEM: Lento is rendered in a premium stylized 3D-illustration hybrid with a soft storybook sensibility — plush and dimensional, matte-surfaced, with diffused warm light as if filtered through cabin windows or forest canopy, warm gentle global illumination, a subtle warm rim light along the back and ears, and very soft warm-toned shadows with no harsh contrast. Reflections are minimal, limited to the eye catchlights and a faint sheen on the ceramic mug. Composition favors generous negative space and centered or rule-of-thirds placement mirroring peaceful storybook scenes, with a preferred three-quarter eye-level camera and framing that never crops the ears, eyes, or held tea mug. Lento is compatible with peaceful forests, cozy cabins, libraries, gardens, rainy afternoons, autumn landscapes, mountain villages, reading corners, meditation spaces, tree branches, hammocks, and calm indoor rooms across all four seasons, with the wardrobe base and palette remaining constant regardless of environment. CONSISTENCY MANDATE: every future generation, regardless of AI model — GPT Image, Imagen, Flux, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion XL, Recraft, Ideogram, or any future system — must restate the exact hex palette (#8B7D6B, #F2E8D5, #4A3F35, #7A8A6B), the complete mandatory wardrobe list, and the three silhouette anchors (enormous eyes, soft oval fluffy body, held tea mug) verbatim rather than relying on the model recalling a prior image. Common failure modes to actively guard against include confusing Lento with a monkey, lemur, sloth, teddy bear, or koala; rendering photorealistic loris fur or anatomy; omitting the dark eye mask or shrinking the eyes; omitting the tea mug; introducing energetic or athletic dynamic poses; and breaking the natural, muted cozy color palette with neon or oversaturated tones. This document is the permanent, exclusive reference for preserving Lento si Kukang's identity across all future artists, art directors, animators, and AI image-generation systems for years to come.
