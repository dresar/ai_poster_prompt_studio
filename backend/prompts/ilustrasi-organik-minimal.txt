GAYA VISUAL REFERENSI: Ilustrasi Organik Minimal
ID: style-010
TEMA WARNA & ESTETIKA: Tema Terang Putih & Abu-Abu Muda Clean minimalis

PANDUAN PROMPTING LENGKAP:
VISUAL PHILOSOPHY: A soft, human, hand-drawn-adjacent illustration language inspired by contemporary editorial nature illustration and slow-living brand identities — organic curved shapes replace geometric precision, celebrating imperfection and warmth over engineered structure. DESIGN OBJECTIVE: Make everyday habits, wellbeing, and lifestyle topics feel approachable, natural, and unhurried. COMPOSITION RULES: Loosely balanced compositions built from overlapping organic blob and leaf-like shapes; a single simplified subject (a plant, a cup, a simplified figure in a natural pose, a sun/moon motif) sits within a cluster of supporting organic forms; asymmetric but visually settled, never chaotic. WHITE SPACE RULES: Generous surrounding space (35%+) so the organic cluster feels like it's floating calmly rather than crowding the frame. GRID SYSTEM: Loose, intuitive placement grid — alignment exists at a macro level (subject roughly centered or rule-of-thirds) but individual shapes are allowed organic freedom within that zone. TYPOGRAPHY HIERARCHY: Rounded or slightly irregular humanist sans or a soft serif headline (e.g. a rounded sans like Quicksand at moderate weight, or a warm serif), gentle-weight body text; occasional hand-lettered-feeling accent word for warmth (kept legible, not overly decorative). FONT WEIGHT: Medium headline, Regular body. ALIGNMENT: Centered or gently left-aligned text beneath or beside the organic illustration cluster. MARGINS: Generous, soft margins framing the composition like a nature journal page. SPACING: Relaxed spacing between text and illustration, no rigid snapping. ILLUSTRATION PHILOSOPHY: Flat solid-color organic shapes with gently imperfect, slightly hand-drawn contour lines (subtle wobble in curves rather than mathematically perfect arcs); layered leaf, cloud, wave, and blob forms build the scene; simplified human figures (if present) use soft rounded forms, no hard joints, gentle curved limbs suggesting ease and calm. OBJECT PLACEMENT: Organic shapes overlap naturally like layered paper cutouts, largest shape anchoring the composition with smaller accent shapes (a small leaf, a dot, a simple sun) scattered with intentional casualness. SUBJECT PLACEMENT: Central but never perfectly symmetrical — slight organic offset feels more natural. LIGHTING: None literal; warmth suggested through color choice alone. DEPTH: Achieved through simple overlap and a very limited two-tone shading trick within larger shapes (a slightly darker organic patch suggesting a fold or shadow, flat-filled, no gradient). MATERIAL: Soft matte, paper-cutout or gouache-painting-inspired flat finish. TEXTURE RULES: Optional very subtle paper-grain or brush-texture overlay at low opacity to reinforce the hand-crafted feel. SOLID COLOR RULES: Warm earthy palette — terracotta, sage green, cream, muted mustard, soft clay pink — always flat and matte. ALLOWED COLORS: Desaturated warm earth tones. FORBIDDEN COLORS: Bright saturated primaries, cold clinical greys/blues as dominant tones, neon. SHAPE LANGUAGE: Organic blobs, leaf shapes, soft waves, rounded-off rectangles — no hard geometric precision anywhere in the illustration. ICON SYSTEM: Small organic accent marks (a simple sun, a leaf, a droplet) in the same flat matte palette. HUMAN ILLUSTRATION RULES: Figures are soft-bodied, rounded, calm in posture, minimal facial detail (at most simple closed-eye calm expression), representing ease rather than dynamic action. BACKGROUND RULES: Solid warm cream or pale sage field, occasionally a soft organic shape bleeding to the edge as a background accent. FOREGROUND RULES: One illustration cluster and headline text only. VISUAL HIERARCHY: Illustration cluster draws the eye first, headline second, body caption last. EDITORIAL BALANCE: Calm, settled asymmetry. INFORMATION HIERARCHY: One lifestyle concept or habit explored per composition. POSTER ADAPTATION: Illustration cluster centered with headline beneath in generous space. INSTAGRAM ADAPTATION: Square format, cluster and text balanced with wide soft margin. CAROUSEL ADAPTATION: Consistent palette and shape language across slides, each introducing a new small organic accent. BANNER ADAPTATION: Illustration right, headline left with soft spacing between. PRESENTATION ADAPTATION: Title slide uses the organic cluster as a calm corner or side accent. BOOK COVER ADAPTATION: Illustration cluster as the full warm cover image with centered title. THUMBNAIL ADAPTATION: Simplified single shape (the anchor blob) must remain recognizable at small size. BRAND CONSISTENCY: Same warm palette and organic contour language throughout. LOGO PLACEMENT: Small, bottom margin, single warm tone. ACCESSIBILITY: Ensure text contrast against the warm cream background remains sufficiently legible. PREMIUM APPEARANCE: Achieved through consistent warmth and intentional imperfection rather than sterile precision. CONSISTENCY: Palette, shape wobble amount, and figure style locked across the entire set.

PROMPT ENGINE V2 — AI DESIGN DIRECTOR SYSTEM. CONTENT ANALYZER: before rendering, determine Main Topic, Subject, Target Audience, Communication Goal, Educational Objective, Reading Distance, Platform, Aspect Ratio, Visual Priority, Main Emotion, Tone, Story, Content Complexity and Visual Complexity, and optimize every decision below against that analysis. SCENE GENERATOR: never render an isolated object — build a small scene, e.g. a rounded figure watering a small tree for growth; a figure meditating beside a soft wave for calm. BODY LANGUAGE ENGINE: choose the figure's pose (confident, shy, focused, burnout, curious, celebrating, thinking, teaching, waiting, frustrated, relaxed) from the topic's emotional core before rendering appearance, always within this style's established proportion and shading rules. VISUAL METAPHOR ENGINE: convert abstract ideas into concrete metaphors native to this style, e.g. growth as seed-to-leaf, renewal as sunrise behind a rounded hill. OBJECT LIBRARY: supporting props drawn only from this style's set: leaf, sun blob, water droplet, potted plant, soft cup, rounded book. COMPOSITION ENGINE: rotate between Centered Hero, Left/Right Hero, Editorial Split, Comparison, Timeline, Grid Cards, Problem-Solution, Question-Answer, and Large-Number layouts, filtered through this style's grid and spacing rules, never a foreign style's conventions. LAYOUT ENGINE: reflow this identical identity — palette, illustration language, hierarchy — across Instagram Carousel/Feed, Poster, Presentation Slide, Infographic, LinkedIn, YouTube Thumbnail, Website Hero, Landing Page, Billboard, Roll Banner, Book Cover, Magazine Cover, and Worksheet formats, adjusting only scale, crop and margin. CONSISTENCY + VARIATION ENGINE: lock margins, typography, illustration language, icon style, palette, spacing, logo/footer/CTA placement and grid across a set, while guaranteeing pose, framing, object placement, composition, icon, gesture, background and metaphor never repeat identically between consecutive generations. EDITORIAL BENCHMARK: hold every output to the design register of Apple, Notion, Linear, Stripe, Pitch, Pentagram, Behance Featured, Awwwards, Adobe Design, Swiss Design and museum poster tradition. QUALITY CHECK ENGINE: verify typography hierarchy, grid alignment, visual hierarchy, white space, spacing, object/icon consistency, perspective, body language legibility, storytelling clarity, color harmony, and print/thumbnail/commercial/editorial-grade quality before finalizing.
