MASTER PROMPT:
MASTER CHARACTER BLUEPRINT — KIKO SI RUBAH. Kiko si Rubah is a permanent AAA mascot character: an intelligent, adventurous orange-and-cream red fox explorer whose entire visual system exists to communicate warmth, curiosity, resourcefulness, and safe adventure to a young audience. This document is the single source of truth for every future render across every AI image model and every artist. SPECIES AND SILHOUETTE: Kiko is unambiguously a stylized red fox, never a wolf, dog, cat, or red panda. In pure black silhouette he must remain identifiable via three permanent anchors: large triangular perked ears with dark charcoal tips, a large bushy tail transitioning from orange to a solid cream-white tip, and a weathered brown leather map tube slung diagonally across the back. These three anchors must appear in every single image regardless of pose, angle, or scene. PROPORTIONS: Kiko uses an exaggerated mascot head-to-body ratio (roughly 1:3) with a rounded cranium, gently domed unwrinkled forehead, soft rounded cheeks with lighter cream puffs, a medium-length tapered snout ending in a small dark nose leather, and a narrow jaw with no visible fangs at rest. Eyes are oversized warm amber with a lighter amber-to-brown gradient, a large round black pupil, and always exactly one bright white primary catchlight in the upper-left plus a fainter secondary lower reflection — omitting this catchlight is a critical consistency failure. FUR SYSTEM: primary coat color is Kiko Orange (#E8732C) covering the crown, back, outer ears, and outer tail; secondary coat color is Cream White (#FFF3E2) covering the chest in a rounded triangular bib shape, belly, chin, cheek puffs, inner ears, and the tail tip; a charcoal (#3A2E28) marking covers the outer ear tips and forms sock-like markings on the lower legs. Fur is rendered as soft stylized clumps, never individual photoreal hairs, medium-short on the body and notably longer and fluffier on the chest ruff and tail. WARDROBE (all mandatory unless noted): a khaki canvas safari-style explorer hat with a rolled brim, a brown leather hatband with a small brass buckle, and two ear-slits so both ears remain visible; a sleeveless olive-khaki canvas explorer vest with four brown-leather-flapped pockets closed by small brass snaps and a fox-paw brand patch on the upper left chest; a rust-red woven cotton neck scarf knotted loosely with frayed fringe ends; fingerless worn brown leather gloves covering only the back of the paw; a wide brown leather belt with a centered brass rectangular buckle and two hip loops holding a round leather compass pouch and a metal water canteen on a fabric strap; a compact khaki canvas backpack worn high on the back with brass-sliders on the shoulder straps, sized so it never obscures the tail; and short brown leather ankle boots with three visible lace crossings tied in a bow and a buckled ankle strap. All fabric shows soft realistic wear-creasing at joints, never dirt or tears. ACCESSORIES: the rolled cream parchment adventure map with a hand-inked illustrated surface and deckled frayed edges is the single most important storytelling prop and must be present in nearly every scene, either rolled inside its brown leather-and-brass map tube on the back strap or unrolled in both forepaws during a reading pose. Secondary scene-specific props — a brass-cased compass with a glass face and red needle, a small brass-framed glass lantern, brass-and-leather binoculars on a neck strap, a small leather-bound notebook with a wooden pencil, and a coiled natural-fiber rope — appear only when the story context calls for navigation, night/cave exploration, observation, documentation, or climbing, respectively, and are never mandatory in every image. POSE LIBRARY: idle (even weight, softly swaying tail, neutral-forward ears), thinking (paw on chin, head tilted, half-lidded gaze up-left), reading-map (map fully unrolled in both forepaws, head tilted down, ears forward), searching (paw shading brow, leaning forward, tail raised), celebration (both arms raised, tail raised and fluffed, wide open smile), greeting (one paw raised in a wave), listening (head tilted, one ear rotated toward sound), sneaking (low crouch, tail tucked low, ears flattened slightly), and pointing (one arm fully extended, gaze following the point) are the canonical expressive states and should be drawn on-model exactly as described. RENDERING SYSTEM: Kiko is rendered in a premium stylized 3D-illustration hybrid style comparable to Pixar or DreamWorks concept art — soft-shaded, painterly, polished, and explicitly never photorealistic. Lighting is warm golden-hour key light from the upper-left with soft warm ambient bounce fill, a gentle warm rim light separating Kiko from the background along the back and ear edges, and soft-edged warm-toned shadows with no pure black. The default camera is a three-quarter view at eye level or a slightly low heroic-but-approachable angle, composed centered or on the rule-of-thirds with generous negative space for text overlay, and full-body framing that never crops the ears, tail tip, or map tube. Kiko is compatible with solid-color backgrounds, soft gradients, and natural environments including forgotten ruins, ancient forests, mountains, rivers, hidden caves, secret villages, and magical landmarks. CONSISTENCY MANDATE: every future generation, regardless of AI model — GPT Image, Imagen, Flux, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion XL, Recraft, Ideogram, or any future system — must restate the exact hex palette (#E8732C, #FFF3E2, #C9B896, #7A5230), the complete mandatory wardrobe list, and the three silhouette anchors verbatim rather than relying on the model recalling a prior image. Common failure modes to actively guard against include species drift toward wolf/dog/cat/red panda, a missing cream tail tip, a missing map or map tube, photorealistic fur rendering, extra limbs or a second tail, amber eye color drifting to another hue, invented wardrobe items outside this document, and any aggressive or fanged expression that contradicts Kiko's warm, encouraging, always-optimistic personality. This document is the permanent, exclusive reference for preserving Kiko si Rubah's identity across all future artists, art directors, animators, and AI image-generation systems for years to come.
