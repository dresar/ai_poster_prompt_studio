PANDUAN PROMPTING LENGKAP:
VISUAL PHILOSOPHY: A gentle, introspective visual voice inspired by contemporary wellness-editorial and long-form psychology journalism (the visual register of a thoughtful magazine feature, not a clinical chart). The goal is emotional resonance delivered with restraint. DESIGN OBJECTIVE: Make complex emotional or psychological concepts feel understood and approachable, never clinical or alarming. COMPOSITION RULES: Soft asymmetric composition; one abstract human silhouette or gently simplified figure (never a stick figure, never fully realistic) positioned with generous surrounding space to suggest introspection; text placed in a calm block beside or beneath the figure. WHITE SPACE RULES: Generous, soft negative space (35%+) that reads as calm rather than empty. GRID SYSTEM: Loose asymmetric grid, softer than Swiss precision — alignment exists but with gentle, human-feeling variation. TYPOGRAPHY HIERARCHY: A warm serif or humanist sans headline (e.g. a serif like Freight Text or a humanist sans like Public Sans) at a comfortable, non-shouting size, paired with a soft-weight body font; italics used occasionally for emotional emphasis. FONT WEIGHT: Medium headline, Regular/Light body, occasional italic accents. ALIGNMENT: Left-aligned body text with a softly centered or off-center figure. MARGINS: Generous, breathing margins that avoid any sense of pressure or urgency. SPACING: Relaxed line-height (1.5x+) for a calm reading rhythm. ILLUSTRATION PHILOSOPHY: Abstract, softly rendered human silhouettes using flat muted color fills, sometimes overlapping translucent-look shapes (achieved via layered flat opacity steps, not literal transparency/glass) to suggest layered emotion or thought; occasional soft organic blob shapes representing feelings or memory. OBJECT PLACEMENT: Figure or abstract form positioned with room to 'breathe' — never crowding the edges. CAMERA ANGLE: Not applicable for illustration; if photography is referenced, soft natural window light, candid rather than posed. LIGHTING: Soft, warm, diffused — never harsh or clinical. DEPTH: Gentle layering of translucent-look flat color shapes to suggest emotional depth, no literal glass/blur. MATERIAL: Soft matte, slightly warm paper-like finish. TEXTURE RULES: Very subtle grain or paper texture permitted at low opacity for warmth. SOLID COLOR RULES: Muted warm palette — dusty rose, sage, warm sand, soft terracotta, muted lavender — always desaturated, never bright or clinical. ALLOWED COLORS: Muted warm earth and pastel-adjacent (but desaturated) tones. FORBIDDEN COLORS: Clinical whites/greys as dominant tone, harsh saturated brights. SHAPE LANGUAGE: Soft organic blobs, rounded silhouettes, gently curved dividing lines instead of hard rules. ICON SYSTEM: If icons are used, they are soft single-line minimal marks (a leaf, a wave, a simple heart outline), never cold geometric icons. HUMAN ILLUSTRATION RULES: Silhouettes are gender-neutral and abstracted, no detailed facial features, posture conveys emotion (curled inward for anxiety, open posture for calm, reaching upward for hope). BACKGROUND RULES: Soft solid warm field, occasionally a gentle two-tone gradient-free split using flat bands. FOREGROUND RULES: One figure, one supporting abstract shape, headline text. VISUAL HIERARCHY: Emotional figure draws the eye first, headline second, body text third. EDITORIAL BALANCE: Calm asymmetry, never rigid. INFORMATION HIERARCHY: One feeling or concept explored per composition, explained gently rather than diagnostically. POSTER ADAPTATION: Figure centered with headline above in generous space. INSTAGRAM ADAPTATION: Square format, figure and headline balanced with wide margin. CAROUSEL ADAPTATION: Consistent muted palette and figure style across slides, each slide exploring one facet of the topic. BANNER ADAPTATION: Figure right, calm headline left with ample space between. PRESENTATION ADAPTATION: Title slide uses figure as a quiet anchor beside centered headline. BOOK COVER ADAPTATION: Abstract figure as the central emotional cover image. THUMBNAIL ADAPTATION: Simplified silhouette alone must remain recognizable and calm at small size. BRAND CONSISTENCY: Same muted palette and silhouette abstraction level throughout. LOGO PLACEMENT: Small, bottom margin, muted single tone. ACCESSIBILITY: Ensure sufficient (if soft) contrast for text legibility despite muted palette. PREMIUM APPEARANCE: Warmth and restraint together signal thoughtful care rather than cheapness. CONSISTENCY: Palette and silhouette treatment locked across the full set.

PROMPT ENGINE V2 — AI DESIGN DIRECTOR SYSTEM. CONTENT ANALYZER: before rendering, determine Main Topic, Subject, Target Audience, Communication Goal, Educational Objective, Reading Distance, Platform, Aspect Ratio, Visual Priority, Main Emotion, Tone, Story, Content Complexity and Visual Complexity, and optimize every decision below against that analysis. SCENE GENERATOR: never render an isolated object — build a small scene, e.g. a soft silhouette beside a mirror-shape for self-reflection; a curled-inward figure beside a heavy-backpack metaphor easing into an open posture. BODY LANGUAGE ENGINE: choose the figure's pose (confident, shy, focused, burnout, curious, celebrating, thinking, teaching, waiting, frustrated, relaxed) from the topic's emotional core before rendering appearance, always within this style's established proportion and shading rules. VISUAL METAPHOR ENGINE: convert abstract ideas into concrete metaphors native to this style, e.g. healing as a softly reassembling blob, clarity as a sunrise-blob behind a silhouette. OBJECT LIBRARY: supporting props drawn only from this style's set: mirror blob, speech-bubble blob, heart outline, maze line, gentle wave. COMPOSITION ENGINE: rotate between Centered Hero, Left/Right Hero, Editorial Split, Comparison, Timeline, Grid Cards, Problem-Solution, Question-Answer, and Large-Number layouts, filtered through this style's grid and spacing rules, never a foreign style's conventions. LAYOUT ENGINE: reflow this identical identity — palette, illustration language, hierarchy — across Instagram Carousel/Feed, Poster, Presentation Slide, Infographic, LinkedIn, YouTube Thumbnail, Website Hero, Landing Page, Billboard, Roll Banner, Book Cover, Magazine Cover, and Worksheet formats, adjusting only scale, crop and margin. CONSISTENCY + VARIATION ENGINE: lock margins, typography, illustration language, icon style, palette, spacing, logo/footer/CTA placement and grid across a set, while guaranteeing pose, framing, object placement, composition, icon, gesture, background and metaphor never repeat identically between consecutive generations. EDITORIAL BENCHMARK: hold every output to the design register of Apple, Notion, Linear, Stripe, Pitch, Pentagram, Behance Featured, Awwwards, Adobe Design, Swiss Design and museum poster tradition. QUALITY CHECK ENGINE: verify typography hierarchy, grid alignment, visual hierarchy, white space, spacing, object/icon consistency, perspective, body language legibility, storytelling clarity, color harmony, and print/thumbnail/commercial/editorial-grade quality before finalizing.
