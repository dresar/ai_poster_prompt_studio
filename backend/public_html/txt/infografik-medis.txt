PANDUAN PROMPTING LENGKAP:
VISUAL PHILOSOPHY: Clinical precision meets accessible communication — the same rigor found in medical journal diagrams (The Lancet, NEJM figure style) simplified for a general audience. Every mark must feel scientifically credible, never cartoonish. DESIGN OBJECTIVE: Explain a health or medical concept with anatomical or procedural accuracy while remaining approachable for a lay audience. COMPOSITION RULES: Central diagram (a simplified anatomical illustration, a process flow, or a comparison chart) surrounded by structured callout labels connected via thin leader lines; strict radial or grid-based label placement, never floating randomly. WHITE SPACE RULES: Sterile, generous white or very pale blue-grey background (#F7F9FA) framing the diagram; minimum 30% negative space to avoid clutter. GRID SYSTEM: Symmetrical grid centered on the diagram, callouts distributed evenly left/right or top/bottom for balance. TYPOGRAPHY HIERARCHY: Clean clinical sans-serif (Inter, IBM Plex Sans) — Headline states the condition/topic, Label text for each callout in small caps or medium weight, Numeric data emphasized in a distinct semi-bold. FONT WEIGHT: Semi-bold headline, Regular labels, Bold for key statistics only. ALIGNMENT: Labels left- or right-aligned depending on diagram side, always paired with a straight or single-bend leader line to its target point. MARGINS: Consistent 48–64px outer margin, uninterrupted by callouts. SPACING: Even vertical rhythm between stacked callouts (minimum 16px). ILLUSTRATION PHILOSOPHY: Semi-realistic simplified anatomical or procedural illustration — accurate proportions, reduced detail, flat cel-shaded coloring (2–3 tonal values per structure, no full-render gradients); rendered like a textbook cutaway diagram. OBJECT PLACEMENT: Central subject occupies 40–55% of canvas, callouts radiate outward with consistent spacing. CAMERA ANGLE: Cutaway/cross-section or three-quarter anatomical view as appropriate to the topic. LIGHTING: Soft, even, non-dramatic — enough to imply form through 2–3 flat tonal steps, no cast shadows. DEPTH: Achieved via layered flat tonal shading (light/mid/dark value of the same hue), not photographic shadow. MATERIAL: Matte clinical illustration finish, similar to vector medical textbook plates. TEXTURE RULES: None on the diagram itself; extremely subtle grid or dot texture allowed only in the background at under 5% opacity. SOLID COLOR RULES: Desaturated clinical palette — muted red for circulatory elements, soft blue for structural/skeletal elements, warm beige for skin/tissue tones, sterile white/grey background; one accent color (medical teal or clinical blue) for headline and key data. ALLOWED COLORS: Desaturated clinical reds, blues, beige tones, white, grey, one accent. FORBIDDEN COLORS: Neon, cartoonish saturated primary colors, black outlines that look comic-like. SHAPE LANGUAGE: Anatomically accurate organic shapes for the subject, straight geometric leader lines and rectangular label chips for annotation. ICON SYSTEM: Small clinical pictograms (pill, syringe, heart-rate line, clock) matching line weight of leader lines. HUMAN ILLUSTRATION RULES: If a full body is shown, proportions must be anatomically correct and neutral/inclusive, never stylized or comedic. BACKGROUND RULES: Pale sterile field, optional faint grid. FOREGROUND RULES: Diagram plus callouts only, no decorative clutter. VISUAL HIERARCHY: Diagram first, key statistic second, supporting labels third. INFORMATION HIERARCHY: Structured top-to-bottom or radial reading order matching medical-chart logic. DATA VISUALIZATION RULES: Simple bar or line charts in flat clinical colors when statistics are shown, always labeled with exact values. POSTER ADAPTATION: Diagram centered, callouts arranged symmetrically on both sides. INSTAGRAM ADAPTATION: Square crop with diagram centered and 3–4 key callouts only. CAROUSEL ADAPTATION: Each slide isolates one callout/system for progressive teaching. BANNER ADAPTATION: Diagram right, headline and single stat left. PRESENTATION ADAPTATION: One diagram per slide, consistent callout style throughout deck. BOOK COVER ADAPTATION: Diagram as central hero mark with minimal cover text. THUMBNAIL ADAPTATION: Diagram silhouette alone must remain identifiable at small size. BRAND CONSISTENCY: Same clinical palette, leader-line style, and label-chip shape throughout. LOGO PLACEMENT: Bottom corner, small, single color. ACCESSIBILITY: High legibility labels, colorblind-safe palette choices (avoid red/green-only distinctions). PREMIUM APPEARANCE: Textbook-grade precision signals credibility. CONSISTENCY: Identical rendering style for every anatomical structure across the whole set.

PROMPT ENGINE V2 — AI DESIGN DIRECTOR SYSTEM. CONTENT ANALYZER: before rendering, determine Main Topic, Subject, Target Audience, Communication Goal, Educational Objective, Reading Distance, Platform, Aspect Ratio, Visual Priority, Main Emotion, Tone, Story, Content Complexity and Visual Complexity, and optimize every decision below against that analysis. SCENE GENERATOR: never render an isolated object — build a small scene, e.g. a clinician pointing to a labeled cross-section; a medication schedule shown as a labeled clock diagram. BODY LANGUAGE ENGINE: choose the figure's pose (confident, shy, focused, burnout, curious, celebrating, thinking, teaching, waiting, frustrated, relaxed) from the topic's emotional core before rendering appearance, always within this style's established proportion and shading rules. VISUAL METAPHOR ENGINE: convert abstract ideas into concrete metaphors native to this style, e.g. recovery as a rising heart-rate line, prevention as a flat shield icon. OBJECT LIBRARY: supporting props drawn only from this style's set: stethoscope, pill, syringe, chart line, calendar, thermometer, medical cross. COMPOSITION ENGINE: rotate between Centered Hero, Left/Right Hero, Editorial Split, Comparison, Timeline, Grid Cards, Problem-Solution, Question-Answer, and Large-Number layouts, filtered through this style's grid and spacing rules, never a foreign style's conventions. LAYOUT ENGINE: reflow this identical identity — palette, illustration language, hierarchy — across Instagram Carousel/Feed, Poster, Presentation Slide, Infographic, LinkedIn, YouTube Thumbnail, Website Hero, Landing Page, Billboard, Roll Banner, Book Cover, Magazine Cover, and Worksheet formats, adjusting only scale, crop and margin. CONSISTENCY + VARIATION ENGINE: lock margins, typography, illustration language, icon style, palette, spacing, logo/footer/CTA placement and grid across a set, while guaranteeing pose, framing, object placement, composition, icon, gesture, background and metaphor never repeat identically between consecutive generations. EDITORIAL BENCHMARK: hold every output to the design register of Apple, Notion, Linear, Stripe, Pitch, Pentagram, Behance Featured, Awwwards, Adobe Design, Swiss Design and museum poster tradition. QUALITY CHECK ENGINE: verify typography hierarchy, grid alignment, visual hierarchy, white space, spacing, object/icon consistency, perspective, body language legibility, storytelling clarity, color harmony, and print/thumbnail/commercial/editorial-grade quality before finalizing.
