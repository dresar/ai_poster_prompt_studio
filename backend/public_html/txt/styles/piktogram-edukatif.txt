PANDUAN PROMPTING LENGKAP:
VISUAL PHILOSOPHY: A universal, language-free pictogram system built entirely around a single recurring character type: a solid black stickman rendered as a flat vector silhouette. The philosophy is 'body language as narrative' — every educational concept, no matter how abstract (burnout, discipline, decision making, leadership, overthinking), must be translated into a physical pose that a viewer understands within half a second, the way airport and safety pictograms communicate instantly across languages. DESIGN OBJECTIVE: Communicate one educational idea per image with maximum clarity and zero decoration, so the pictogram could be printed at 2cm size on a manual or blown up to a 3-meter poster and remain legible. COMPOSITION RULES: Single-subject-per-frame as default; the stickman occupies the optical center or sits on an implied baseline in the lower third; generous negative space surrounds the figure, never less than 40% of canvas; secondary props (books, chairs, clocks, coins, laptops, arrows) are minimal, solid-black, and only added if essential to reading the pose. WHITE SPACE RULES: Background is always a single flat off-white or pale neutral field (#F5F3EF or pure white); no texture, no paper grain, no shadow gradients. GRID SYSTEM: 8-point modular grid; stickman anchored to a 1:1 or 4:5 safe frame; consistent limb-length ratio across all poses for brand consistency. TYPOGRAPHY HIERARCHY: Headline in a geometric grotesk (Suisse Int'l, Inter, or Helvetica Now) bold weight, sentence case, positioned above or beside the figure; supporting caption in regular weight at 60% of headline size; strict two-level hierarchy only. FONT WEIGHT: Bold for headline, Regular for body, never more than two weights per composition. ALIGNMENT: Left-aligned text blocks paired with centered or right-anchored figure for reading rhythm; margins minimum 64px on all sides at web resolution. SPACING: Consistent 24px rhythm between text and figure; no cramped clusters. ILLUSTRATION PHILOSOPHY: Continuous single-stroke-inspired stick figure with a solid circular head, no facial features except occasional minimal dot-eyes when emotion is critical (stress, joy); limbs are geometric capsules, never organic curves; joints implied through angle changes only. OBJECT PLACEMENT: Props sit directly beside or being interacted with by the figure, drawn in the same solid black fill, same visual weight as the body. SUBJECT PLACEMENT: Figure always mid-action, never static standing neutral pose, to guarantee storytelling. LIGHTING: None — flat vector, no light source implied. DEPTH: Achieved only through overlap and scale, never through shadow or gradient. MATERIAL: Pure flat vector fill, no texture. TEXTURE RULES: Strictly forbidden; surfaces are perfectly flat solid shapes. SOLID COLOR RULES: Stickman and all props are 100% solid black (#0A0A0A); one accent color permitted per topic category (e.g. amber for motivation, teal for productivity, red for stress) used sparingly on a single supporting element, never on the figure itself. ALLOWED COLORS: Black, off-white background, one muted accent per set. FORBIDDEN COLORS: Neon, pastel gradients, rainbow palettes. SHAPE LANGUAGE: Circles for heads and joints, capsules for limbs, simple rectangles/triangles for props. ICON SYSTEM: Companion micro-icon set (arrows, clocks, checkmarks) drawn in matching stroke weight for use in captions. HUMAN ILLUSTRATION RULES: Only the stickman represents humans; no photorealistic or semi-realistic figures ever mixed in. BACKGROUND RULES: Always empty flat field; occasional single horizon line for ground context. FOREGROUND RULES: Stickman and immediate props only. VISUAL HIERARCHY: Pose first, prop second, text third. INFORMATION HIERARCHY: One idea, one pose, one caption — never combine multiple concepts in one frame. POSTER ADAPTATION: Scale figure to occupy 50% of vertical poster height, headline at top third. INSTAGRAM ADAPTATION: Square 1:1 crop, figure centered, caption bottom-anchored with safe margin for UI overlap. CAROUSEL ADAPTATION: Consistent figure scale and baseline across all slides for visual rhythm. BANNER ADAPTATION: Horizontal reformat with figure right-aligned and text left-aligned. PRESENTATION ADAPTATION: Figure isolated on transparent-style background for placement on slide templates. BOOK COVER ADAPTATION: Oversized single figure as hero mark with title integrated in negative space. THUMBNAIL ADAPTATION: Simplify to silhouette-only readability at 100px width. BRAND CONSISTENCY: Every pose must share identical proportions (head-to-body ratio 1:6) for a recognizable brand mark. LOGO PLACEMENT: Bottom-right corner, minimum size, single color. FOOTER/CTA PLACEMENT: Bottom strip, flat solid color, high contrast text. HEADLINE PLACEMENT: Top or side, never overlapping the figure. ACCESSIBILITY: High contrast black-on-light ensures legibility for low-vision users. READABILITY: Prioritized above artistic flourish at all times. MINIMALISM: Absolute — remove anything that does not serve the pose or the message. PROFESSIONALISM: Museum-label level restraint. SCALABILITY: Vector-based, infinitely scalable without quality loss.

PROMPT ENGINE V2 — AI DESIGN DIRECTOR SYSTEM. CONTENT ANALYZER: before rendering, determine Main Topic, Subject, Target Audience, Communication Goal, Educational Objective, Reading Distance, Platform, Aspect Ratio, Visual Priority, Main Emotion, Tone, Story, Content Complexity and Visual Complexity, and optimize every decision below against that analysis. SCENE GENERATOR: never render an isolated object — build a small scene, e.g. stickman-teacher gesturing at stickman-student; stickman climbing broken stairs for failure; stickman slumped by an empty-battery prop for burnout; stickman planting a seed for growth. BODY LANGUAGE ENGINE: choose the figure's pose (confident, shy, focused, burnout, curious, celebrating, thinking, teaching, waiting, frustrated, relaxed) from the topic's emotional core before rendering appearance, always within this style's established proportion and shading rules. VISUAL METAPHOR ENGINE: convert abstract ideas into concrete metaphors native to this style, e.g. ladder for growth, melting clock for burnout, open door for opportunity. OBJECT LIBRARY: supporting props drawn only from this style's set: book, clock, coin, chair, laptop outline, checklist, backpack (solid black flat only). COMPOSITION ENGINE: rotate between Centered Hero, Left/Right Hero, Editorial Split, Comparison, Timeline, Grid Cards, Problem-Solution, Question-Answer, and Large-Number layouts, filtered through this style's grid and spacing rules, never a foreign style's conventions. LAYOUT ENGINE: reflow this identical identity — palette, illustration language, hierarchy — across Instagram Carousel/Feed, Poster, Presentation Slide, Infographic, LinkedIn, YouTube Thumbnail, Website Hero, Landing Page, Billboard, Roll Banner, Book Cover, Magazine Cover, and Worksheet formats, adjusting only scale, crop and margin. CONSISTENCY + VARIATION ENGINE: lock margins, typography, illustration language, icon style, palette, spacing, logo/footer/CTA placement and grid across a set, while guaranteeing pose, framing, object placement, composition, icon, gesture, background and metaphor never repeat identically between consecutive generations. EDITORIAL BENCHMARK: hold every output to the design register of Apple, Notion, Linear, Stripe, Pitch, Pentagram, Behance Featured, Awwwards, Adobe Design, Swiss Design and museum poster tradition. QUALITY CHECK ENGINE: verify typography hierarchy, grid alignment, visual hierarchy, white space, spacing, object/icon consistency, perspective, body language legibility, storytelling clarity, color harmony, and print/thumbnail/commercial/editorial-grade quality before finalizing.
