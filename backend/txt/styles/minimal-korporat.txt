GAYA VISUAL REFERENSI: Minimal Korporat
ID: style-004
TEMA WARNA & ESTETIKA: Tema Terang Putih & Abu-Abu Muda Clean minimalis

PANDUAN PROMPTING LENGKAP:
VISUAL PHILOSOPHY: Quiet confidence. Minimal Korporat borrows from contemporary fintech and consulting-firm visual identities — restrained neutral palettes, generous breathing room, and typography that signals trust rather than excitement. Every decision favors calm authority over visual noise. DESIGN OBJECTIVE: Make the audience feel they are reading a report from a credible, established institution — clarity and trust over spectacle. COMPOSITION RULES: Centered or left-anchored single-column layouts with wide symmetrical margins; content organized in clean horizontal bands; one supporting graphic element per composition (a thin-line chart, a simple geometric badge, or a subtle icon) placed with generous isolation. WHITE SPACE RULES: Minimum 45% empty space; whitespace is the primary luxury signal. GRID SYSTEM: Simple 4 or 6 column grid with wide gutters; strict vertical rhythm using consistent line-height multiples. TYPOGRAPHY HIERARCHY: Refined serif or humanist sans headline (e.g. a serif like Tiempos or a humanist sans like Söhne) at moderate size — never shouting — paired with a clean grotesk body (Inter, Helvetica); three levels: Headline, Supporting line, Fine-print caption. FONT WEIGHT: Medium for headline, Regular for body, Light for captions — subtlety over boldness. ALIGNMENT: Predominantly centered for hero statements, left-aligned for body copy blocks. MARGINS: Wide, symmetrical, generous outer margins (10%+ of canvas). SPACING: Airy vertical spacing between sections, never cramped. NEGATIVE SPACE: Used deliberately to convey premium restraint. ILLUSTRATION PHILOSOPHY: Extremely minimal — thin single-weight line icons or one small abstract geometric mark (a circle, a subtle arc, a thin bar chart); no character illustration, no cartoon figures. OBJECT PLACEMENT: One small graphic element positioned with large isolation, often near the headline as a supporting badge. LIGHTING: Flat, no directional light; if a photograph is used, soft even studio lighting with muted tones. DEPTH: Subtle layering through very light tonal shifts (5–10% tint differences), never strong shadow. MATERIAL: Matte, paper-like, corporate-stationery feel. TEXTURE RULES: None — perfectly flat or extremely subtle paper-grain texture at very low opacity if warmth is needed. SOLID COLOR RULES: Neutral base palette — ivory, warm grey, deep navy, charcoal — with one muted accent (sage green, dusty blue, or burnt terracotta) used sparingly on a single element. ALLOWED COLORS: Muted neutrals plus one desaturated accent. FORBIDDEN COLORS: Bright saturated hues, neon, multi-color palettes. SHAPE LANGUAGE: Thin circles, subtle rounded rectangles, minimal geometric badges. ICON SYSTEM: Single-weight thin-line icon set, consistent stroke width across all marks. BACKGROUND RULES: Solid neutral field, occasionally split into two calm tonal bands. FOREGROUND RULES: Headline, one supporting line, one small graphic mark only. VISUAL HIERARCHY: Headline first, thin graphic mark as quiet anchor, caption last. EDITORIAL BALANCE: Symmetrical, calm, centered weight distribution. INFORMATION HIERARCHY: One core statement, one supporting statistic in fine print. POSTER ADAPTATION: Headline centered in upper half, generous lower whitespace with small logo mark. INSTAGRAM ADAPTATION: Centered composition with large margin frame. CAROUSEL ADAPTATION: Consistent neutral background across all slides, accent color reserved for key slide only. BANNER ADAPTATION: Left-aligned headline with right-side whitespace reserved for CTA. PRESENTATION ADAPTATION: Title slide uses centered headline on neutral field with thin rule line beneath. BOOK COVER ADAPTATION: Refined serif title centered on textured neutral field. THUMBNAIL ADAPTATION: Headline word plus subtle mark must remain legible small. BRAND CONSISTENCY: Same neutral palette and accent hue across all touchpoints. LOGO PLACEMENT: Small, centered top or bottom, generous clear space around it. CTA PLACEMENT: Understated outlined button, no fill, thin border. ACCESSIBILITY: Sufficient but soft contrast, prioritizing calm over stark contrast. PREMIUM APPEARANCE: Whitespace and restraint are the primary premium signals. CONSISTENCY: Identical margin ratios and accent placement logic across the entire set.

PROMPT ENGINE V2 — AI DESIGN DIRECTOR SYSTEM. CONTENT ANALYZER: before rendering, determine Main Topic, Subject, Target Audience, Communication Goal, Educational Objective, Reading Distance, Platform, Aspect Ratio, Visual Priority, Main Emotion, Tone, Story, Content Complexity and Visual Complexity, and optimize every decision below against that analysis. SCENE GENERATOR: never render an isolated object — build a small scene, e.g. a consultant reviewing a thin-line chart at a clean desk; two silhouettes shaking hands. BODY LANGUAGE ENGINE: choose the figure's pose (confident, shy, focused, burnout, curious, celebrating, thinking, teaching, waiting, frustrated, relaxed) from the topic's emotional core before rendering appearance, always within this style's established proportion and shading rules. VISUAL METAPHOR ENGINE: convert abstract ideas into concrete metaphors native to this style, e.g. growth as a thin ascending arc, trust as two overlapping thin circles. OBJECT LIBRARY: supporting props drawn only from this style's set: thin-line chart, simple badge mark, slim document or handshake icon. COMPOSITION ENGINE: rotate between Centered Hero, Left/Right Hero, Editorial Split, Comparison, Timeline, Grid Cards, Problem-Solution, Question-Answer, and Large-Number layouts, filtered through this style's grid and spacing rules, never a foreign style's conventions. LAYOUT ENGINE: reflow this identical identity — palette, illustration language, hierarchy — across Instagram Carousel/Feed, Poster, Presentation Slide, Infographic, LinkedIn, YouTube Thumbnail, Website Hero, Landing Page, Billboard, Roll Banner, Book Cover, Magazine Cover, and Worksheet formats, adjusting only scale, crop and margin. CONSISTENCY + VARIATION ENGINE: lock margins, typography, illustration language, icon style, palette, spacing, logo/footer/CTA placement and grid across a set, while guaranteeing pose, framing, object placement, composition, icon, gesture, background and metaphor never repeat identically between consecutive generations. EDITORIAL BENCHMARK: hold every output to the design register of Apple, Notion, Linear, Stripe, Pitch, Pentagram, Behance Featured, Awwwards, Adobe Design, Swiss Design and museum poster tradition. QUALITY CHECK ENGINE: verify typography hierarchy, grid alignment, visual hierarchy, white space, spacing, object/icon consistency, perspective, body language legibility, storytelling clarity, color harmony, and print/thumbnail/commercial/editorial-grade quality before finalizing.
